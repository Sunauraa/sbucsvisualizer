Study notes

Words to know

LIFO
Last In, First Out, like a pile of plates. Stacks are LIFO.
FIFO
First In, First Out, like a checkout line. Queues are FIFO.
stack
a LIFO list. push(x) adds on top, pop() removes the top, top() (or peek) looks at it without removing it.
queue
a FIFO list. enqueue(x) joins at the rear, dequeue() leaves from the front, first() looks at the front.
deque
“double-ended queue”, said “deck”. You can add and remove at both ends.
overflow
adding to a structure that is already full (here, all 8 array slots are in use).
underflow
removing from (or looking into) an empty structure.

Cost and uses

Each cell is time / extra space, worst case. The array holds exactly 8 slots, so you can hit the “full” error.
