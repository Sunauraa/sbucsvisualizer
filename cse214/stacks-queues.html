Stacks, Queues & Deques

Three restricted lists, each implemented on a plain array. The restriction is the point: allowing access only at the ends makes every operation O(1). The queue shows the one catch, and why it needs a circular array.

Why circular?

Array queue strategyenqueuedequeueproblem
front pinned at index 0O(1)O(n)every dequeue shifts n − 1 elements
front index moves, no wrapO(1)O(1)freed slots are never reused: “full” with empty space
circular (front + size, mod N)O(1)O(1)none. Every slot is reusable.

The circular array keeps the cheap “move an index” dequeue of the second strategy, and fixes its flaw by letting the index wrap from N − 1 back to 0. Tracking size as well as front tells a full queue apart from an empty one, because both have front == (front + size) mod N.
