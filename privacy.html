Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

DSA Visualizer is a free study site with interactive visualizers for data structures and algorithms. This page explains what it stores about you, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. In short: without an account it stores nothing about you on any server, and with an account it stores only what it needs to show you your own progress.

If you don't sign in

You can use every visualizer, the code playground, the quizzes and the study notes without an account. Nothing about you is sent to us. Your settings (for example, code language and which boxes are open), your quiz score and the code you write in the playground are saved only in your own browser, so they're there next time on that computer. The site also keeps a copy of its own pages in your browser so it works offline. You can clear all of this at any time through your browser's settings ("site data").

The site uses no cookies for tracking, no analytics and no advertising.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It lets the site keep your progress and show it on every computer you use.

What we receive from Google

When you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address, profile picture link and an account identifier, and nothing else. We don't receive your password, and we can't see your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account. The site shows only your name and email (in the account menu).

What we store

  • Your account: the details above, so you can sign in again.
  • Your progress: for each module, which tabs you opened, which operations you ran, and how many quiz questions you answered and got right.
  • Your LeetCode status: the status you set on each practice problem (To do, Solved, Revisit). We don't connect to your LeetCode account.
  • Your notes: the text you write, and the animation steps the note is pinned to.

How we use it

Only to show you your own progress, LeetCode status and notes, and to keep them in sync between your computers. We don't sell it, rent it, share it with advertisers, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

DSA Visualizer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see your data

  • You. The database only lets a signed-in student read and change their own rows. Other students can't see your progress or notes.
  • The site's maintainer has administrator access to the database, as anyone running a service does, and uses it only to keep the service working (for example, to fix a problem you report).
  • Service providers that run the site for us: Supabase stores the accounts and the database, and Vercel hosts the website. Like any web host, they keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect their services. They process data on our behalf and under their own privacy policies.

We don't share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Links to other sites

Practice problems link to LeetCode, and some pages link to other sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies. We don't send them anything about you.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Sign out (account menu) removes your data from the computer you're on. It stays in your account for next time.
  • Delete my account (account menu) permanently deletes your account, progress, LeetCode status and notes from the database straight away. It can't be undone.
  • Otherwise we keep your data for as long as you have an account.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Signing in uses Google's OAuth with PKCE, so the site never handles a password. Access to every student table is limited by database rules to the student who owns the rows.

Children

The site is meant for university students. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly let children under 13 create accounts.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. If a change affects what we store or who can see it, we'll say so on the site before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions or requests (for example, a copy of your data) go through the feedback form.
